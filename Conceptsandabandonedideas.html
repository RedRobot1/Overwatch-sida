Gamemodes
campaign (PVE) Abandoned

The Overwatch Campaign was originally planned as a PvE (Player vs. Environment) experience that would expand the Overwatch universe through story-driven missions, hero progression, and cooperative gameplay. Players would team up to fight Null Sector, uncover the secrets behind the Omnic crisis, and explore the personal stories of their favorite heroes. However, in 2023, Blizzard shifted away from the original vision, citing development challenges and the difficulty of sustaining the ambitious scope of the project. Although some story missions were released in Overwatch 2, the larger campaign and its promised talent trees and replayable progression systems were ultimately abandoned, leaving much of the planned experience unrealized.

Heroes
Project Titan Abandoned

Overwatch grew out of Blizzard's cancelled MMO project, Titan. Some of the ideas and characters were reworked into the hero shooter we know today.

Hero Talents (PvE) Abandoned

Overwatch 2 was planned to have talent trees for upgrading heroes in PvE missions. The plans for the full PvE campaign were scaled back and the talents were not released as first planned.
